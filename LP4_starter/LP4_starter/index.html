A Coastline Built on Light

Wisconsin's two Great Lakes coastlines are home to more than thirty historic lighthouses, many still standing since the 1800s.

Before radio and GPS, these towers were the only thing standing between a ship and disaster on Lake Michigan's rocky shoals.

Today, most are preserved as museums, and a handful still guide boats home every night.

Lighthouses Worth the Drive

Rawley Point Light

Located in Point Beach State Forest, this is one of the tallest lighthouses on the Great Lakes.

Cana Island Light

Reachable by a short causeway walk, Cana Island is the most photographed lighthouse in Door County.

Split Rock Light

Perched on a cliff above Lake Superior, this lighthouse is one of the most visited attractions on the North Shore.

Eagle Bluff Light

Set inside Peninsula State Park, this restored keeper's residence gives visitors a look at 1800s lighthouse life.

Planning a lighthouse road trip? The Wisconsin Historical Society publishes a free downloadable map of every public lighthouse in the state.
